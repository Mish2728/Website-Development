Pizza

Pizza is a globally beloved Italian dish consisting of a flattened, leavened wheat dough base topped with combinations of tomato sauce, cheese, and various savory ingredients, traditionally baked at extreme temperatures in wood-fired ovens.

Did You Know?

Pizza is the most popular food around the whole world!

It takes 30 minutes to make a perfectly baked homeade pizza

Cooking Style

Preheat your oven to 500°F (260°C) and stretch 1 pound of pizza dough into a 12-inch circle on parchment paper. Spread ½ cup of tomato sauce, 1½ cups of mozzarella, and your favorite toppings over the dough, leaving a small border. Bake for 10 to 15 minutes until the crust is golden-brown and the cheese is bubbly, then slice and serve hot.
