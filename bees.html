LET'S LEARN SOME INTERESTING ABOUT BEES

Bees are flying insects belonging to the clade Anthophila, with over 20,000 known species found on every continent except Antarctica. They feed exclusively on flower nectar and pollen, acting as vital pollinators for a large portion of the world's wild plants and food crops

ABOUT BEEKEEPERS

Beekeepers manage colonies of honeybees in artificial hives to collect honey, beeswax, and other hive products, as well as to provide essential crop pollination.
